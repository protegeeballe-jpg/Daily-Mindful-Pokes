Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Faith & Focus: Daily Armor (the “App”) handles information when you use the web application. The App is maintained by the owner of the public project repository. It is designed to keep routine and writing records in your own browser rather than in an operator-managed account or central server.

1. Information stored by the App

The App stores information locally in your browser using browser storage. Depending on the features you use, this can include:

  • App access settings: a salted password-derived verifier for the local app PIN. The App does not store the PIN itself as plain text.
  • Checklist and progress: selected dates, checklist completion states, guided-step states, released-cloud states, completion markers, and confidence points.
  • Garden information: your selected tree and flower, planting date, and watering dates.
  • Private writing: diary entries and the four checklist reflection fields. These are encrypted in the browser with AES-256-GCM. A separate passphrase is processed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 to derive the encryption key. The key is held in memory while the writing is unlocked; the passphrase is not saved by the App.
  • Compressed local cache: to support low-memory mobile devices, local app data is compressed using the native browser `CompressionStream` (gzip) API when you explicitly request a backup.

2. What is and is not encrypted

The app PIN is a local screen gate; it does not encrypt ordinary checklist progress, confidence points, or garden information. Those records remain in browser storage in their normal data format. Private diary entries and checklist reflections are stored separately as encrypted records.

For enhanced session security, the App features an inactivity auto-lock timer (automatically locking private writing after 5 minutes of idle time) and a background visibility lock (immediately dropping active encryption keys from memory when switching apps or locking the device screen).

Choose a strong, memorable private-writing passphrase and keep it safe. The App cannot recover private writing if that passphrase is lost. Clearing browser or site data can permanently remove local records unless backed up to Google Drive.

3. Google account and Google Drive data

Optional Google Drive Backup & Restore: The App provides optional, user-initiated cloud backup and restore functionality powered by Google Identity Services (OAuth 2.0).

When you choose to connect your Google account, the App requests permission to access the restricted appDataFolder scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata). This scope allows the App to save and retrieve a single compressed backup file (faith-focus-armor-backup.gz) within a private, hidden app folder on your personal Google Drive.

Privacy Guarantees for Google Data:

  • Strictly Manual Sync: Cloud backup and restore actions are 100% user-initiated (clicking "Back Up This Device" or "Restore From Drive"). The App does not perform automatic background uploads or background syncing.
  • No Access to General Drive Files: The App cannot read, modify, view, or access any other files, folders, or documents stored in your Google Drive account.
  • No External Server Transmission: Backup data is sent directly between your device browser and Google Drive API endpoints. No backup data or authentication tokens are ever sent to an operator server or third party.
  • PIN Exclusion: Your local device app PIN verifier is intentionally excluded from the backup payload to preserve local access security boundaries.

The App remains fully usable locally without connecting a Google account.

4. Microphone and voice notes

If you choose the voice-recording control, the browser asks for microphone permission. Audio is used to create a temporary in-page preview for that session. The App does not upload that recording to an operator server or save it as a permanent App record.

5. Where information is processed

Routine, garden, PIN-verifier, and encrypted-writing records are processed in your browser and stored on your device. The App does not operate a user account database or an operator-managed application server for this information.

The public website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub provides the website hosting and may process ordinary connection information under its own terms and privacy notices. The App's local records are not sent to GitHub by the App's ordinary local features.

6. Sharing, sale, and advertising

The App maintainer does not sell personal information and does not use App records for advertising. The App does not transmit diary entries, checklist progress, or garden records to third parties. All cloud features communicate strictly and directly with your personal Google Drive account upon manual request.

7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain in your browser until you remove them, clear the site's stored data, or use an App control that deletes the relevant record. Clearing site data may delete the app PIN verifier, checklist history, confidence points, garden records, and encrypted writing. You can use the manual Google Drive backup feature to preserve your compressed data before clearing browser storage.

8. Children's privacy and family use

The App can be used locally without creating an online account. A parent or guardian should supervise a child's use and should consider what personal or sensitive information is entered into a shared device.

9. Security

The App uses browser-native Web Crypto API encryption ($AES-256-GCM$) for private writing, PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 key derivation (600,000 iterations), automatic session locking on inactivity or background hide, and a local PIN gate. No browser-based safeguard can guarantee protection if a device, browser profile, or operating system is compromised. Keep your device updated, use a device lock, and do not share your private-writing passphrase.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. The updated version and effective date will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or the App's data practices, contact the project maintainer through the project's GitHub issue page. Do not post passphrases, PINs, diary text, access tokens, or other private information in a public issue.
