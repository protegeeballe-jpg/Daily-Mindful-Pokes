Privacy Policy

Faith & Focus: Daily Armor (the “App”) helps you manage daily routines, personal reflections, voice notes, location check-ins, and a small garden. This policy explains what information the App uses, where it is kept, and what happens when you use backup and synchronization features.

1. Account information

To use an account, you choose a username and a passphrase. The App does not ask for your email address or phone number. Your passphrase must be at least 12 characters long.

When creating an account, you also choose three security questions and provide answers. Keep a private record of the questions you selected and the exact answers you entered. They may be needed to recover a forgotten username or reset your passphrase. If you lose your passphrase and cannot provide the required answers, the App's maintainer cannot restore your account or private records.

2. Information the App stores

Depending on which features you use, the App may store:

  • Checklist and progress: the dates you use the App, completed and incomplete activities, and progress information.
  • Diary and reflections: your diary entries and private checklist reflections.
  • Voice notes: audio you choose to record, along with the date and information needed to play it.
  • Location check-ins: a location and time record when you choose a location check-in.
  • Little Garden: your selected plants and their progress.
  • Account details: your username and information needed to provide account access and recovery.

3. Where your information is kept

The App saves information in your browser so you can use it on your device. Diary entries and voice notes receive additional protection before they are saved. Some checklist, progress, location, and Garden information is stored in the browser without encryption. This means someone who can access your device or browser profile may be able to see some of that information.

The App also prepares encrypted daily backup copies for synchronization and export. These copies are protected before they leave your browser. This protection does not mean that every item saved locally by your browser is encrypted.

For your privacy, use a device and browser profile that you control. Keep your device locked when you are away, and do not use a shared or public computer for personal entries.

4. Cloud synchronization and service providers

When cloud synchronization is available, the App automatically sends encrypted daily backup copies to its cloud storage service so your information can be kept in sync across devices. The service receives the protected backup copy, not readable Diary entries or voice recordings. The provider may still process ordinary technical information needed to operate the service, such as connection details and synchronization activity.

The public App pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Cloud synchronization uses services provided by Cloudflare. These providers handle technical information under their own terms and privacy policies. The current App does not use Google Drive or GitHub Gist for backup.

Synchronization may not complete if your device or browser closes before it finishes. Changes made to the same date on different devices may also need your attention. Keep an encrypted backup of important information and review both copies when the App reports a conflict.

5. Backups, reset, and deletion

Settings offers an optional encrypted JSON backup that you can save and later import into the same account. Keep the exported file somewhere private. Anyone who obtains the file may be able to keep it, even if you later remove the copy from your device.

The Health Checklist Reset clears checklist progress and reflections for the selected date and restarts the Garden planting setup. It keeps your main Diary entries, voice notes, and location history.

The current App does not provide a control to delete cloud backups by date or remove an entire account. Clearing your browser data removes local copies from that browser, but it does not delete cloud backups. Keep this in mind before clearing browser storage or changing devices.

6. Microphone and location

The App asks for microphone permission only when you choose to record a voice note. Recordings receive additional protection in the browser before they are saved or included in a backup.

The App requests your current location only when you choose a location check-in. Your check-in is kept in your history and included in the protected daily backup. If you open or share a location using another service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

7. Children and family use

Families may use the App together. A parent or guardian should guide a child's use, especially on a shared device, and should consider what personal information is entered. Do not share account details with anyone who should not have access to the account.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may change when the App or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page. For questions, contact the project maintainer through the project's GitHub issue page. Do not post passphrases, recovery answers, Diary entries, location details, or other private information in a public issue.
