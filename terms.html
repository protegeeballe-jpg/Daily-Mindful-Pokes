4. Your information and device security

The App stores its routine, garden, and writing records in browser storage on your device. Checklist and garden records are not encrypted by the App. The local app PIN serves as a screen gate, and features an inactivity auto-lock timer and background visibility lock for enhanced session protection. Private diary text, checklist reflections, and saved voice notes are encrypted with a separate passphrase. Voice recordings are stored in the browser's IndexedDB storage and are included as encrypted records in optional manual Google Drive or GitHub Gist backups. The App also offers manual GitHub Gist synchronization: the entire supported backup payload is encrypted in the browser with a separate Gist backup passphrase before upload through the Cloudflare Worker. The Cloudflare Sync Key authorizes the Worker and is distinct from the backup passphrase. Google Drive remains an independent optional backup. You are responsible for keeping your device secure and protecting your passphrases and Sync Key.

When you tick a place checkbox on the first checklist card, your browser may request a one-time current location. Each successful capture is appended to a read-only, date-specific location history displayed in the unlocked Diary. The coordinates, selected place, reported accuracy, and capture time are stored separately in browser storage on that device. Coordinates can be copied to the device clipboard or opened in Google Maps from the Diary and checklist details. Opening the map sends the coordinate query to Google Maps under its applicable privacy practices. The App provides no control to edit or delete these location records, and the Health Checklist Reset does not remove them. They are not encrypted at rest and are not included in GitHub Gist or Google Drive backups. Location access depends on your browser and device permissions. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser storage may be able to access locally stored records.

The Health Checklist Reset clears checklist progress and checklist reflection fields for the selected date and restarts the Garden planting setup. It preserves the main Personal Diary entries, saved voice notes, and location history. If you forget the private-writing passphrase, the App cannot recover the encrypted writing or voice notes. Clearing site data, changing browsers, or replacing a device may remove or make local records unavailable unless restored via Google Drive backup. Location history is excluded from Google Drive backup and cannot be restored from that backup.
