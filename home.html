Faith & Focus: Daily Armor

A calm space for daily routines, reflection, and steady growth

Build your day with faith and focus.

Faith & Focus: Daily Armor is a local-first wellness and reflection web app with a username-based encrypted vault. It brings simple daily activities, gentle progress tracking, a growing garden, and a private writing space together in one place.

Daily Health Checklist

Work through six activity cards, guided steps, and daily progress at your own pace.

The Little Garden

Choose a fruit tree and a flower, then watch their stages of growth follow the calendar.

Personal Diary

Your Diary shares the same encrypted account key as the Health Checklist and Garden. Sign in once to access your private writing.

Personal Progress

See your daily confidence points and celebrate completing the checklist.

Local-first by design with enhanced privacy. The app keeps live routine, Garden and writing records locally on your device, and stores encrypted daily packets in IndexedDB. Each daily bundle is compressed and encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a permanent account master key protected by your passphrase and your three selected recovery answers.

Automatic encrypted vault sync. A Cloudflare Worker stores each account’s encrypted daily packets in isolated Cloudflare KV keys. The Worker never receives your passphrase or account master key. A protected Account Directory supports sign-in and three-question recovery, while Durable Objects coordinate account identity and cross-device updates.

Optional local JSON backup. Settings lets you export or import encrypted daily packets as a JSON file. Automatic Cloudflare sync requires no manual backup buttons.
