Faith & Focus: Daily Armor

A calm space for daily routines, reflection, and steady growth

Build your day with faith and focus.

Faith & Focus: Daily Armor is a personal, local-first wellness and reflection web app. It brings simple daily activities, gentle progress tracking, a growing garden, and a private writing space together in one place.

Daily Health Checklist

Work through six activity cards, guided steps, and daily progress at your own pace.

The Little Garden

Choose a fruit tree and a flower, then watch their stages of growth follow the calendar.

Personal Diary

Keep private reflections protected with a separate passphrase and browser-based encryption.

Personal Progress

See your daily confidence points and celebrate completing the checklist.

Local-first by design with enhanced privacy. The app stores its routine, garden, and writing records locally on your device, utilizing automatic Gzip compression for optimal performance. Private diary and reflection fields are encrypted using your separate private-writing passphrase, protected by an inactivity auto-lock timer and background session security.

Encrypted GitHub Gist sync. You can manually save and restore a client-side encrypted backup through the Faith & Focus Cloudflare Worker. A separate backup passphrase stays in your browser; the GitHub token stays in a Cloudflare Secret. Secret Gists are unlisted, not private, so the entire backup is encrypted before upload.

Optional Google Drive backup. Google Drive remains a separate, independent backup option. It can be used alongside Gist sync and is not replaced or disabled by it. All cloud operations are user-initiated; there are no background uploads.
