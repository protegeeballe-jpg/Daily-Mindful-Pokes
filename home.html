Faith & Focus: Daily Armor

A calm space for daily routines, reflection, and steady growth

Build your day with faith and focus.

Faith & Focus: Daily Armor is a personal, local-first wellness and reflection web app. It brings simple daily activities, gentle progress tracking, a growing garden, and a private writing space together in one place.

Daily Health Checklist

Work through six activity cards, guided steps, and daily progress at your own pace.

The Little Garden

Choose a fruit tree and a flower, then watch their stages of growth follow the calendar.

Personal Diary

Keep private reflections protected with a separate passphrase and browser-based encryption.

Personal Progress

See your daily confidence points and celebrate completing the checklist.

Local-first by design. The app stores its routine, garden, and writing records in this browser on your device. Checklist and garden records are not encrypted by the app. The diary and reflection fields are encrypted separately using your private-writing passphrase.

Google Drive backup is currently paused. The app does not automatically connect to Google, request Google sign-in, or send app data to Google Drive. Any future backup is intended to be optional and user-initiated, never automatic or performed in the background.
