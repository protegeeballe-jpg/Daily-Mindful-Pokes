Faith & Focus: Daily Armor

A calm space for daily routines, reflection, and steady growth

Build your day with faith and focus.

Faith & Focus: Daily Armor brings your daily wellness checklist, personal reflections, voice notes, and Little Garden together in one welcoming space. Use it at your own pace and make it part of your daily routine.

Daily Health Checklist

Work through six activity cards, guided steps, and daily progress at your own pace.

The Little Garden

Choose a fruit tree and a flower, then watch their stages of growth follow the calendar.

Personal Diary

Write private reflections in a personal space protected by your account.

Personal Progress

See your daily progress and celebrate each step you complete.

Your information stays close to you. The App saves information in your browser. Diary entries and voice notes receive additional protection, while some checklist, progress, location, and Garden information is stored locally without encryption. Use a device and browser profile that you control.

Encrypted cloud backups. When cloud synchronization is available, the App sends protected daily backup copies to help keep your information in sync across devices. Settings also offers an optional encrypted file backup.

For details about information stored by the App, account recovery, and backup limits, please read the Privacy Policy.
